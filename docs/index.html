How to Use Foldex

No heavy software to launch. No file upload limits. Convert and compress directly where your files live.

01

Select Your Files

Highlight any image or PDF directly in Windows File Explorer. Works seamlessly with single files or bulk multiple-file selections.

Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF & more
02

Press Shortcut or Right-Click

Press Shift+C to compress, or Shift+V to convert. You can also right-click and pick Foldex from the context menu.

Instant popup appears at your cursor
03

Configure & Process

Select your target format, quality slider, or DPI preset in the compact window. Click process — output files appear instantly beside originals without overwriting.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Foldex and how your files are handled.

Does Foldex upload my files to any cloud server?
No. Foldex operates strictly 100% offline. All image processing, compression algorithms, and PDF manipulations execute locally on your machine using your CPU. Zero telemetry, zero uploads, and no network calls.
Can I customize the keyboard shortcuts?
Yes. While the default shortcuts are Shift+C for compression and Shift+V for conversion, you can easily change them to any hotkey combination you like in the Foldex Settings panel.
What file formats are supported?
Foldex provides full multi-format conversion between JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF, and PDF documents. Transparency is preserved on compatible image formats, and multi-page PDFs can be generated from multiple selected images or split into high-res images at 72, 150, or 300 DPI.
Will Foldex ever overwrite my original files?
Never. Foldex enforces a non-destructive guarantee. All generated and compressed files are saved alongside your source files with automated numbered collision renaming (e.g., image (1).png) or to an output directory of your choice.
