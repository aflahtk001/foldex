Frequently Asked Questions

Does Foldex upload my files to any cloud server?

No. Foldex is completely offline. All image compression, PDF creation, and conversions take place strictly on your local computer CPU.

Can I customize the keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. While the defaults are Shift+C for compression and Shift+V for conversion, you can change them to any combination inside Foldex Settings.

What formats are supported?

Foldex supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF, and PDF documents.
